FAQ

Before you
apply.

Who is the mentorship for?+

It is designed for traders who want more structure around strategy, execution, risk, and discipline. Your application helps determine whether 1-on-1 mentorship is appropriate for your current stage.

Do I need trading experience?+

No specific amount of experience is required to apply. The application asks where you are currently so the mentorship can be evaluated for fit.

Is this really 1-on-1?+

Yes. The offer promoted on this site is private 1-on-1 mentorship with Zander rather than a large pre-recorded course library.

Does mentorship guarantee profitability?+

No. Trading is risky and there is no guaranteed outcome. Mentorship can provide education, structure, and feedback, but every trader is responsible for their own decisions and risk.

Is ZTRADEZ affiliated with Alpha Futures?+

Yes. ZTRADEZ is an Alpha Futures affiliate. Affiliate relationships may provide compensation for referrals. That relationship does not guarantee funding, payouts, or trading success.

Can I join the Discord without mentorship?+

Yes. You can join the ZTRADEZ Discord directly from the links on this page.
