01The approach

The goal is not more signals. The goal is better decisions.

A repeatable process should answer four questions before you ever click buy or sell. Mentorship is about making those answers yours, written down, and followed.

  1. 01What am I looking for?
  2. 02When do I stay out?
  3. 03What invalidates the idea?
  4. 04How much am I willing to risk?

02Private 1-on-1

Not another content library.

Mentorship is built around the trader in front of Zander: what you trade, where your process breaks down, and what needs to become more consistent.

01

Strategy

Define the setups you are actually looking for instead of reacting to every move.

02

Execution

Build clearer rules around entries, invalidation, exits, and when not to trade.

03

Risk

Structure size, loss limits, and trade management around protecting capital first.

04

Discipline

Reduce impulsive decisions with a routine you can repeat under pressure.

The difference

Direct feedback. Specific to you.

The point of 1-on-1 mentorship is not to copy someone else's trades. It is to understand the logic behind a process and build the discipline to execute your own rules.

05Honest fit check

Is this for you?

Private mentorship works best when expectations line up on day one. Read both lists before you apply.

A strong fit if you

  • Want to trade futures from written rules instead of gut feel.
  • Are willing to journal your trades and review them honestly.
  • Want direct feedback on your own execution, not a feed of calls.
  • Treat risk limits as non-negotiable, even on good days.

Probably not a fit if you

  • Are looking for signals or trades to copy.
  • Expect guaranteed payouts or a shortcut to a funded account.
  • Are not ready to put in screen time and review.
  • Want to size up before you have a process worth sizing.

13FAQ

Before you apply.

Still have a question? Ask it in the Discord and someone from ZTRADEZ will point you in the right direction.

Ask in Discord ↗
Who is the mentorship for?

Traders who want more structure around strategy, execution, risk, and discipline. Your application helps determine whether 1-on-1 mentorship is appropriate for your current stage.

Do I need trading experience?

No specific amount of experience is required to apply. The application asks where you are now so the mentorship can be evaluated for fit.

Is this really 1-on-1?

Yes. The offer on this site is private 1-on-1 mentorship with Zander, not a large pre-recorded course library.

How long does mentorship last?

Lifetime. Once you're accepted, your access to mentorship with Zander doesn't expire.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is shared after your application is reviewed, so it can be discussed in the context of where you are. There is no cost to apply.

Does mentorship guarantee profitability?

No. Trading is risky and there is no guaranteed outcome. Mentorship can provide education, structure, and feedback, but every trader is responsible for their own decisions and risk.

Is ZTRADEZ affiliated with Alpha Futures?

Yes. ZTRADEZ is an Alpha Futures affiliate. Affiliate relationships may provide compensation for referrals. That relationship does not guarantee funding, payouts, or trading success.

Can I join the Discord without mentorship?

Yes. You can join the ZTRADEZ Discord directly from the links on this page.
