01Who we are

This website is operated by ZTRADEZ, the private futures trading mentorship run by Zander. "ZTRADEZ", "we", "us" and "our" refer to ZTRADEZ. This policy explains what information this website collects, why, and what you can do about it.

02Information you give us

We only collect personal information you choose to send us through the forms on this site.

  • Mentorship application: your name, the country you live in, trading experience, the markets you trade, your biggest challenge, why you want mentorship, the amount you are ready to invest, how you heard about ZTRADEZ, your email address, your Discord username, whether you prefer WhatsApp or phone call and text, and your phone number with its country code.
  • Student review form: the name or handle you want shown, time in mentorship, your rating, your review, your email address, and whether you give permission to publish the review.

03How forms are processed

Both forms are processed and stored by our website host, Netlify, Inc., using Netlify Forms. Submissions are available only to ZTRADEZ through its Netlify account. A copy of each submission is also sent to a private channel in the ZTRADEZ Discord server that only the ZTRADEZ team can see, so new applications and reviews can be answered quickly. Netlify and Discord handle this information under their own privacy policies and security practices.

04Information collected automatically

  • Hosting logs: like most websites, our host may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request, to deliver and secure the site.
  • Fonts: text on this site uses Google Fonts. Your browser requests the fonts from Google, which receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • Videos: YouTube videos load only when you press play, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Once a video loads, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to it.
  • No tracking cookies: this site does not currently use analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies.

05Storage in your browser

After you submit an application, your answers are saved in your browser's session storage so the thank-you page can show them back to you. This copy stays on your device, is not sent anywhere else, and is deleted automatically when you close the browser tab.

06How we use your information

  • To review your application and decide whether mentorship is a good fit.
  • To contact you about your application or mentorship by email, Discord, or the phone method you chose (WhatsApp, or phone call and text).
  • To understand which countries applicants come from, and to check whether mentorship, scheduling across time zones, and payment are practical where you live.
  • To provide mentorship if you become a student.
  • To publish your review on our website or social media, but only if you gave permission. Your email address is never published.
  • To understand which channels bring people to ZTRADEZ.

We do not sell your personal information.

07When we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us run this website and mentorship (such as Netlify for hosting and forms, Discord for receiving submissions and for communication, email, and WhatsApp, operated by Meta Platforms, Inc., if you chose to be contacted there), or when required by law. We never sell your phone number or share it with other businesses for their marketing. If you follow a link to another site, such as Alpha Futures, YouTube, Instagram, or Discord, that site's own privacy policy applies.

08How long we keep it

We keep application and review submissions for as long as needed to review them, provide mentorship, and keep reasonable business records, or until you ask us to delete them.

09Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you sent us. Depending on where you live, such as California or the European Union, you may have additional rights under local law. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

10Children

This website and mentorship are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you send us, and the site is served over an encrypted connection. No method of storing or transmitting information online is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

13Contact
